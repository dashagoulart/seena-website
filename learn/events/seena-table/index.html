More Than a Cooking Workshop

FoodScienceTraditionsHuman connection

Welcome to the Seena Table, where food, science, traditions, and human connection come together.

We’ll explore the art of fermentation using seasonal vegetables, discover the fascinating relationship between our gut and overall well-being, and share food traditions and memories around one table.

Food for Thought

Did you know that approximately 70% of our immune cells are associated with the gut?

In a short, engaging talk, we’ll explore:

  • Gut health and immune function as winter approaches
  • The gut–brain connection, stress, and mood
  • Why many supermarket pickles don’t contain live beneficial bacteria
  • The difference between fermented foods and probiotics

Food for the Gut

Get hands-on and prepare your own jar of naturally fermented seasonal vegetables, without vinegar.

Learn the process, understand the science, and take your jar home to continue fermenting.

Food for the Soul

Food carries memories, cultures, and family traditions.

When you arrive, you’ll be invited to leave a food memory or tradition in our shared jar. Later, we’ll open a few notes and let the stories and conversations unfold naturally.

Sharing is always optional.

Moments Around the Table

A look back at our previous editions

Our Seena Table gatherings bring together curious minds from all walks of life, from lawyers and physicians to entrepreneurs, creatives, parents, and simply those who love food and meaningful connections, around one table to explore fermentation, food traditions, and the gut microbiome.

From preparing our own fermentation jars to sharing stories and memories, it is always a beautiful beginning to the Seena Table community.

Meet Your Host

Gultekin Daglar
Food and Nutrition · Culinary Wellness Educator · Co-Founder & CEO of Seena Integrative Health Centre

With a background in Biomedical Sciences and a degree in Food & Nutrition from Toronto Metropolitan University, Gultekin has 10+ years of experience in nutrition education, community health projects, and integrative wellness.

Through Seena Table, she brings together her professional knowledge and love for cooking, cultural traditions, and meaningful human connection.
