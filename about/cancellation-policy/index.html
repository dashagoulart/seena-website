Cancellation Policy

Please read before booking your appointment.

By booking with Seena Integrative Health Centre, you are agreeing to the following terms and conditions.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

24-hour notice required

We require a minimum of 24 hours' notice for any appointment cancellation or rescheduling request.

Changes made within 24 hours of the scheduled appointment time will incur a fee equivalent to 50% of the missed appointment cost, payable at the time of your next appointment booking.

To cancel or reschedule, please call or text Seena Integrative Health Centre at +351 924 353 219.

No-Show Policy

Missed appointments without notice

In the event of a missed appointment without prior notice, all future appointments must be prepaid in full, including the 50% missed appointment fee.
